Our Story

Sweet Crumbs Bakery started with one simple goal in a small home kitchen: to bake premium desserts that look beautiful and taste absolutely unforgettable.

Today, we blend traditional French pastry techniques with modern cake artistry. Every order is baked in small batches to ensure the highest quality. We never compromise on taste, hygiene, or presentation.

Our Journey

Milestones that define who we are today.

The Home Kitchen

2020

First Retail Store

We opened our doors on MM Alam Road, expanding our menu to include artisan cookies, cupcakes, and a dine-in coffee experience.

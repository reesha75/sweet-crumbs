Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common queries.

How much notice do you need for a custom cake? +
For standard signature cakes, we require at least 24 hours notice. For fully custom and tiered event cakes, we request a minimum of 1 week notice.
Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options? +
Yes! We have a dedicated menu for gluten-free and vegan cupcakes and cookies. Please mention your dietary requirements when ordering.
Do you deliver outside Lahore? +
Currently, we only offer delivery within the greater Lahore area to ensure our delicate desserts arrive in perfect condition.
